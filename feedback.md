# Design Feedback Evaluation

**Date:** October 2026  
**Participants:** Zoë George, Nina, Maryam

---

## 1. Summary by Design Variation

| Design | Prototype Variation | Average Score | Total Reviews | Key Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Design 1** | **Variation A** (Accordion Builder) | **5.0 / 5** | 3 | Highest-rated design. Users loved watching the burger visually grow in real time and appreciated the clear order confirmation summary. |
| **Design 2** | **Variation B** (Step-by-Step Wizard) | **4.5 / 5** | 2 | Liked the structured step-by-step flow, but noted that navigating between steps hid the live burger growth, and tall burgers could cause asset clipping. |
| **Design 3** | **Variation C** (Tabs & Card Grid) | **4.5 / 5** | 2 | Great landscape space usage keeping the burger always in view; evaluators wished for fun illustrations for each ingredient card. |
| **Design 4** | **Variation D** (Split-Screen Layout) | **4.5 / 5** | 2 | Clean, solid layout, but evaluators felt it felt very similar to previous variations and could use more distinct differentiation. |
