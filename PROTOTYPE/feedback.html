Key Usability Insights

  • Design 1 (Variation A) Emerged as User Favorite: Unanimous 5/5 score. Evaluators praised watching the burger grow dynamically in real time and appreciated the clear order confirmation summary.
  • Design 2 (Variation B): Rated 4.5/5. Users liked the step structure, but felt filtering separated the steps too much and obscured the continuous burger growth. Noted potential asset clipping on very tall custom stacks.
  • Design 3 (Variation C): Rated 4.5/5. The landscape layout preserved the burger visibility well, but users requested richer illustrations for ingredients.
  • Design 4 (Variation D): Rated 4.5/5. Good overall impressions, but differentiation from Variation C could be made more distinct.
